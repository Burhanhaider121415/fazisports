Privacy Policy

FAZI SPORTS respects the information you choose to share when contacting us. This Privacy Policy explains, in simple terms, what information we may receive and how we use it.

1. Information You Provide

When you contact FAZI SPORTS about a wholesale inquiry, you may choose to provide information such as your:

  • Name
  • Phone or WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Company or business details
  • Products you are interested in
  • Wholesale requirements or other information included in your message

You only need to provide information relevant to your inquiry.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Discuss products and wholesale requirements
  • Communicate about potential or existing orders
  • Continue relevant business correspondence with you

3. Selling Personal Information

FAZI SPORTS does not sell customers’ personal information.

Information provided through an inquiry is used for communication related to your request and our business relationship with you.

4. Correcting or Removing Your Information

If you believe information you provided is incorrect, or you would like us to remove information we hold about you where reasonably applicable, you can contact FAZI SPORTS using the details below.
